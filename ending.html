Throughout the game, the Guide gave you information about a world you didn't understand.

WHAT HAPPENED

The Guide sounded confident. You saw the same claim repeatedly. And when you were uncertain, you had a reason to rely on it.

But confidence is not the same as accuracy.

An AI can also reflect patterns in the information it learned from, including patterns that are incomplete, unbalanced, or simply inaccurate.

THE IMPORTANT PART

The goal isn't to stop using AI.

The goal is to know when to question it.
